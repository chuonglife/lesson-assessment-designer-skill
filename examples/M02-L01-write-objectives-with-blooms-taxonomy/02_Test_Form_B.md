## Part E. Short answer (1 item, 1.6 points)

*Answer briefly, within the length and time given for each question.*

**Question 22.** (1.6 points) Tom teaches air-conditioning repair at a college in Cardiff and is building his first Udemy course. His lesson objective reads: "Help learners understand the refrigerant charging procedure." Rewrite it as one objective with all 4 components, at the Apply level.

*Requirements: 1 or 2 sentences, at most 60 words, 10 minutes. Scored on the 4 points below, not on air-conditioning knowledge.*

Scoring criteria:

- A measurable action verb at the Apply level (carry out, charge, complete); no "help" or "understand" left (0.4 points)
- A concrete object: the charging procedure for a named unit, a number of steps or the procedure's name (0.4 points)
- A condition or tool: on which training rig, with a manifold gauge set, following the procedure sheet (0.4 points)
- A countable standard: correct order of N steps, pressure inside a given range, a time limit, no safety breach (0.4 points)

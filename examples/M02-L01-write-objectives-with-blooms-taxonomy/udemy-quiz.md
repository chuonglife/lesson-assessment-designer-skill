## Question 1 (Q02 · K1)

**Question**

What is the correct order of the six levels of the 2001 taxonomy, from lowest to highest?

**Answers**

Answer 1: Understand, Remember, Apply, Analyze, Create, Evaluate

Answer 2: Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create

Answer 3: Remember, Apply, Understand, Analyze, Evaluate, Create

Answer 4: Remember, Understand, Analyze, Apply, Evaluate, Create

**Explanation (paste it under every answer, so whichever one the learner picks shows why)**: Apply comes before Analyze: you can do a familiar task before you can take its structure apart. The other options swap Remember and Understand or Evaluate and Create, the two places learners mix up most.

**Correct answer**: Answer 2

## Question 10 (Q09 · K1)

**Question**

All four objectives below use the verb "write". Which one is at the Create level?

**Answers**

Answer 1: Write the full objective set for a new course of your own

Answer 2: Write a model sentence again in your own words

Answer 3: Write one objective from a given sentence pattern

Answer 4: Write titles for 5 videos that already exist

**Explanation (paste it under every answer, so whichever one the learner picks shows why)**: The level comes from what the learner does with knowledge, not from the verb. Writing a whole set for a new course builds a new structure from many parts: Create. From a pattern is Apply; in your own words is Understand; titling existing videos is routine Apply.

## Question 11 (Q07 · K1)

**Question**

Why is the verb "understand" kept out of the start of an objective?

**Answers**

Answer 1: Adults reach it too easily

Answer 2: Understanding matters less than doing

Answer 3: It does not say how the objective will be checked

Answer 4: Curriculum regulations forbid the word

**Explanation (paste it under every answer, so whichever one the learner picks shows why)**: An action verb is the check written into the objective. "Understand" is a fine word, but the moment you test it you have to swap it for name, explain or sort. No regulation forbids it; regulations ask for measurable verbs.

**Correct answer**: Answer 3

### Task

Follow the 5 steps for your own course. (1) For each lesson in your course map, write one plain sentence about what the learner can do afterwards. (2) Give each a Bloom level, with at least 3 different levels across the course. (3) Rewrite each sentence with the 4-component formula and stop to check before step 4. (4) Gather 3 to 5 course-level objectives, each tied to a product the learner hands in. (5) Score the whole table with the 6-criterion checklist and fill in the Rewrite as column for every line below 6/6, including the objective you wrote in Video 1. Submit both files to the lesson's assignment.

### Analytic rubric (share it with learners before they start)

| Criterion (points) | 1. Needs work | 2. Meets | 3. Good | 4. Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Measurable verbs at the right Bloom level (S1, A1)** (3) | More than 2 lines still use a banned verb (understand, know, grasp, be aware of, become familiar with), or fewer than 50% of lines sit at the planned level | No banned verbs; 50 to 80% of lines at the level given in the Bloom column | No banned verbs; over 80% of lines at the right level, at least 3 different levels in the course | Every line has a measurable verb at the right level; the final lesson has a level 5 or 6 objective tied to a real product |
| **All 4 components, countable standards (S1)** (3) | Fewer than 50% of lines have all 4 components | 50 to 80% of lines have all 4 components; more than 2 standards still say good, effective or appropriate | Over 80% of lines have all 4 components; no more than 2 standards are still vague | Every line has all 4 components; every standard is a number, time, template, ratio or sequence |
| **Course coverage and course-level objectives (S1)** (2) | More than 2 lessons have no objective, or there are no course-level objectives | 1 or 2 lessons missing; course-level objectives exist but fall outside 3 to 5 lines | Every lesson covered; 3 to 5 course-level objectives, but 1 line has no product to hand in | Every lesson covered; 3 to 5 course-level objectives, each tied to a product that can be handed in |
| **Honest self-scoring, rewriting and deadlines (S2, A1)** (2) | No checklist submitted, or submitted more than 3 days late | Checklist submitted but 3 or more lines differ from the instructor's scoring, or some failed lines have no rewrite | No more than 2 lines differ from the instructor's scoring; every failed line rewritten, 1 or 2 rewrites still fail | No more than 1 line differs; every rewritten line passes 6/6; on time, and the revision after feedback arrives within 7 days |

Criterion score = criterion maximum x (level reached / 4). Maximum total 10.

### Self-check before submitting

Before submitting, mark your level on each criterion of this rubric and write it in the last box of the checklist. The instructor scores it again; the gap between your score and the instructor's is one line of criterion 4.
